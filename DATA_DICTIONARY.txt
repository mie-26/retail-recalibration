Numerical conventions

H and U are days for M5/Retail and weeks for Dominick’s. relative_loss_pct is 100*(candidate/reference-1); negative is lower loss. Coverage fields ending weak/strict in absolute score files are proportions; contrast fields ending _difference are percentage points where specified by the source table. joint_scaled is the equal-weight q90/q95 score, normalized by fixed series scales. n_pairs counts observed series-origin targets per horizon, not enterprises. lower/upper are descriptive interval endpoints; reference_block marks 2H. Data6 timing values are seconds unless explicitly named minutes. Data1/2 are original and prespecified analyses; Data3 contains post-review controls; Data4 diagnostics are post hoc; Data5/6 are cost/provenance summaries. See each CSV and the JSON field inventory for exact identifiers.

Restored score_data tables (CSV and Parquet) retain per-origin counts and per-quantile scores. Zero-count origins are excluded by observed-count weighting. Scores, not forecasts, are averaged over seeds. Every comparison retains the common available target mask.

Compact scope: enhanced_daily.parquet retains only mode=core and joins the exact original-unit pinball column from the full archive by original row_index. Extended aggregate and daily-stratum arrays are not included. Training-budget and MultiQT daily arrays, full robustness-grid daily arrays and non-core historical daily streams are excluded, with final summaries retained. See SCOPE.json.

Controlled follow-up (Data 8): controlled/E1, E2 and E3 daily tables contain observed count n and per-origin/quantile mean scores. Loss numerators equal mean times n. The JSON dictionary inventories all controlled CSV/Parquet tables. relative_loss_pct is candidate/reference minus one, times 100; negative favors the candidate. Weak/strict/equality/zero_tie scores are proportions; reported coverage differences are percentage points. E2 interaction_pct_of_Raw is the QT sharing difference minus the WQ sharing difference, divided by common Raw loss and times 100. For scalar display intervals, point/lower/upper are relative loss percentages. Selection and support records have distinct units documented in docs/RESULTS_MAP.md.

Italian (Data 9): selected/daily.parquet retains all configurations/seeds for the selected E2 group/series, Raw and feedback-bridge arms. These are per-origin, seed, method and quantile aggregate scores. Candidate daily scores and selected E1/global/sorted daily arms are omitted, while their final numerical summaries remain included. Multiply each mean metric by n for its numerator. Zero-support dates remain in the calendar and have missing means. effect_intervals.relative_loss_percent=100*(candidate/reference-1); interaction_percent is QT group-minus-series minus WQ group-minus-series, divided by Raw loss and times100. Coverage fields are proportions; changes labelled pp are percentage points. E3 new_reference equals R3_isolate; do not count two independent results. Column inventories and row counts are in the root DATA_DICTIONARY.json.
